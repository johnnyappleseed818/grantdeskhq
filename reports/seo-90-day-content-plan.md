# 90-day post-award organic content plan

## Objective

Own the post-award workflow: award → obligations → reporting plan → financial inputs → program inputs → evidence → report → human review → submission/closeout. Google Analytics remains the traffic source of truth; this plan does not rebuild traffic analytics.

## Standard

Publish two original, source-linked pages each week only if they pass quality review. Every time-sensitive claim needs a primary source. Each page needs a stable URL, unique title/description, canonical metadata, legitimate structured data, Resources link, and intent-matched CTA.

## Decision rules

Optimize for qualified signup/activation, not pageviews. Do not publish thin content. Rework or stop a topic that fails source, usefulness, differentiation, or conversion-quality review.
