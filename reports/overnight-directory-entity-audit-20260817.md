# Organic directory and entity-presence audit

Date: 2026-08-17  
Scope: credible, low-cost public listings for GrantDeskHQ. This is a research and preparation artifact only: no listing was created, claimed, edited, purchased, or submitted.

## Decision summary

Prioritize first-party identity surfaces (Apple, Bing, LinkedIn) and use Google Business Profile only if GrantDeskHQ has a real customer-facing location or service-area operation. GrantDeskHQ appears to be an online software product; Google explicitly excludes online-only businesses, lead-generation companies, and brands without in-person customer contact. Do not create a duplicate or an artificial local listing.

## Guardrails and verification checklist

1. One canonical name, website, category, and contact identity across approved profiles; record deviations and their reason.
2. The owner or explicitly authorized representative must control verification and retain admin access. Do not share passwords.
3. Do not publish an address, hours, phone, customer list, review, award, metric, integration, or certification unless the owner has confirmed it.
4. Search each service for an existing entity before creating anything; resolve duplicates through the platform's ownership process.
5. No payment, advertising upgrade, review solicitation, automated submission, or outbound outreach is part of this audit.
