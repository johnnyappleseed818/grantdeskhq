## Cannibalization rules

1. Each primary intent has one canonical URL. Do not create near-identical spelling variants or several generic checklists.
2. A template receives its own page only when the underlying resource exists, has an owner, and is substantive enough to publish.
3. The Resources hub is the public index. Blog articles link back to Resources and forward to the product route that matches intent.
4. Comparison intent remains conditional; no alternative, versus, or competitor keyword page is published until current facts and brand-safe framing are reviewed.
5. Educational pages use low-pressure CTAs. Commercial evaluation pages may link to Pricing and Free First Report.
6. Every published page needs a unique title, description, canonical, stable route, sitemap entry, source links where factual claims need support, and Article or CollectionPage schema only when applicable.
