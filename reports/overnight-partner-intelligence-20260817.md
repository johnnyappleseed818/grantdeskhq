## Classification notes

The inventory intentionally favors A/B records where GrantDeskHQ can support a provider’s existing client service without taking over accounting judgment, grant interpretation, audit responsibility, or submission. C is used where the public offering includes broad technology/process or outsourced-finance capabilities that may overlap. No record is classified D from these pages alone: a D classification would require evidence of a directly competing post-award reporting product or replacement workflow, not merely overlapping nonprofit finance services.
