## Scoring method

Each score is an explainable internal fit score out of 100: nonprofit-focus (25), post-award fit (25), portfolio or community access (25), and public evidence strength (25). Scores rank research priority only. They are not evidence of intent, a relationship, consent, product need, conversion likelihood, or permission to contact.

## Required human-review gate

Before any future, separately approved partner communication:

1. Reconfirm current public source evidence and partner policy.
2. Identify an appropriate role from a permitted, public source.
3. Review service overlap, conflicts, commercial terms, and claims.
4. Draft a specific value proposition without asserting a partnership or referral fee.
5. Obtain any required human approval for outbound activity.

No automation is enabled for these steps.
