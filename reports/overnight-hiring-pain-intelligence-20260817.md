## Prioritization rationale

1. **P0: subaward oversight and closeout evidence.** GAO supplies the strongest quantified, recent public signal; Grants.gov supplies the operational lifecycle and deadline context. These are the best themes for product discovery and hiring-market research.
2. **P1: restricted-fund accounting and reserves visibility.** UK government guidance provides concrete control and reporting obligations. Treat this as a cross-market signal, not a claim about a particular organization or jurisdictional equivalence.
3. **P1: multi-party reporting handoffs.** The Grants.gov reporting chain supports research into roles responsible for collecting, checking, and submitting evidence.

## Observed facts versus inference

- **Observed:** statements quoted or paraphrased above are tied to the linked public source and its publication/update or crawl date.
- **Inference:** pain hypotheses, product fit, buyer personas, priority, and “why now” are analyst judgments based on the observed requirements. They are not admissions by organizations or verified hiring signals.
- No anonymous post, forum comment, or generic pain statement is attributed to a named organization. No organization is presented as a prospect or as having expressed buying intent.
