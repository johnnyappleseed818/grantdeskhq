# Buyer-language intelligence

The intelligence primitive accepts only legitimate public, source-linked observations. Each theme stores phrase, source type, segment, pain category, buyer stage, relevance, evidence count, and last observed timestamp. It is designed to inform human-reviewed messaging, content, partner positioning, and objection handling.

Current initial themes to investigate through approved public signals: spreadsheet bridge work, grant reconciliation, expense allocation, reporting deadlines, reporting requirements, budget-to-actual, supporting documentation, finance/program coordination, and closeout. These are research categories, not asserted customer quotes.
