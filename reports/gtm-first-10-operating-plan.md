# First-10-customer operating plan

## Status vocabulary
**ACTUAL:** observed GrantDeskHQ data. **TARGET:** operating goal. **ASSUMPTION:** planning input that must be replaced with observed conversion data.

## Actual baseline
- Qualified public-award prospects: 20.
- Verified contacts: 0.
- Approved outreach: 0.
- Outreach sent: 0.
- Replies, trials, activations, paid customers, MRR, ARR: 0 observed in this SHADOW workspace.
- Google Analytics is the website-traffic source of truth; this plan does not recreate traffic analytics.

## Milestones
- **TARGET Milestone 1:** 10 paying nonprofit customers.
- **TARGET Milestone 2:** 4 new paying customers per week.

## Funnel metrics
Track qualified prospects; contact-identified; enrichment-ready; verified; suppression clear; drafts ready; human-approved sends; actual sends; replies; positive replies; Free First Award trials; activation; paid customers; MRR; ARR; channel; recent-award signal; persona; and source attribution.

## Planning funnel — assumptions, not performance
For each channel test, start with the smallest controlled cohort allowed by human review. Example measurement math: 25 approved contacts → replies → positive replies → Free First Award trials → activated workflows → paid subscriptions. Do not set conversion rates as facts until GrantDeskHQ has observed results. Record denominator, date range, message/version, channel, award signal, and outcome for every stage.

## Weekly cadence
1. Monday: check inventory targets, source quality, suppression state, and live customers/conversions.
2. Tuesday: founder reviews only small, approved draft cohort; no automatic send action.
3. Thursday: review replies/conversions and Free First Award activation quality.
4. Friday: compare channels by paid activation, not opens/pageviews; select one messaging/content/partner experiment for the next week.

## Thresholds
- Scale a channel only after it produces source-backed positive replies and paid/activated outcomes at acceptable quality; do not scale on opens or pageviews.
- Change message/targeting when a small, human-reviewed cohort gets no meaningful response or when recipients show poor fit.
- Stop a channel when it generates suppression, complaint, relevance, or qualification concerns.
- Do not move a contact past human approval without VERIFIED email and CLEAR suppression.

## Initial allocation — assumptions
Recent-grant outbound 45%; organic/AI search 30%; partners/referrals 15%; Reddit/community manual research 7%; LinkedIn research/drafting 3%. Replace with conversion evidence. No Reddit/LinkedIn action is automated or authorized.

## Channel attribution
Persist lead_id, campaign_id, utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, and utm_content through signup, first-report start, checkout, and subscription. Suppress a converted contact immediately. This is lifecycle attribution, not a replacement for Google Analytics traffic reporting.

## Safety
No send, schedule, CRM export, social action, or contact-form action is defined by this plan. Commercial offer claims must match actual current billing configuration; commercial readiness is currently blocked by production TEST Stripe wiring.
