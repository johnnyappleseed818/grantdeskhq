# Content-to-product internal-link review

## Architecture
Homepage → How It Works → Resources → Guides/templates → Pricing or Free First Report. Each public guide returns to Resources and uses a single intent-matched conversion path.

## Implemented paths
- Resources hub surfaces every published blog URL.
- Published guides use canonical metadata, source links, and an existing self-service CTA.
- Sitemap and llms.txt include the four new public pages on feature/seo-post-award-content.

## Recommended CTAs
- Operational checklist or agreement guide: **Try one award for free**.
- Finance workflow or template: **See how GrantDeskHQ prepares a funder report**.
- Commercial buyer guide: **See self-service plans** or **See how GrantDeskHQ works**.

## Guardrails
Avoid aggressive CTAs in every paragraph, link only to relevant product pages, never promise automated submission/certification, and preserve the distinction between general guidance and award-specific requirements.
