GRANTDESKHQ — AI-NATIVE GTM & DISTRIBUTION ENGINE — PHASE 1 RESULTS
Date: 2026-08-25

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Implemented a founder-approved, non-sending Phase-1 GTM opportunity engine. It turns existing durable award, Direct, Partner, Social, and canonical readiness evidence into explainable Opportunity Clusters rather than another prospect list. It preserves the existing GTM runtime, Instantly execution integration, outbound cohort, content engine, Search Console, and product funnel.

CURRENT SYSTEM MAP
- KEEP: USAspending award scanner; Direct public-signal discovery; Partner public discovery; Social scanner; canonical GTM/prior-contact/suppression model; Hunter waterfall; Instantly adapter and polling; inventory autopilot; Search Console/content engine; Free First Award funnel.
- IMPROVE: market-event interpretation, cross-source clustering, distribution visibility, decision-oriented founder UI, and persisted learning-ready entities.
- NEW: Signal, Opportunity Cluster, source-backed evidence, Distribution Node/edge evidence, configurable score, Distribution Leverage Score, founder-only playbooks and experiment-ready state.
- DEFER: automatic campaigns/sends, partner portal/economics execution, analyzer/calendar implementation, large graph crawler, autonomous optimization. These are later phases and are intentionally not faked.

CURRENT GTM FLOW
Public award/hiring/community/partner signals -> existing qualification and canonical reconciliation -> verified contact/Ready inventory -> separately authorized Instantly batch -> provider polling/outcomes. The new layer reads durable outputs only and creates prioritized market-event clusters for founder review. It cannot stage contacts, mutate campaigns, or send email.

BOTTLENECK ANALYSIS
The prior system already has quality controls and replenishment, but evaluates prospects primarily one at a time. It lacked an explainable market-level object that joins recurring funding/reporting signals, buyer readiness, and distribution routes. The highest leverage gap was founder prioritization: which market event or intermediary should be attacked first, with proof.

PHASE-1 IMPLEMENTATION
- src/lib/gtmOpportunityEngine.ts centralizes score weights: ICP 20, urgency 20, reporting complexity 15, cluster size 15, buyer resolvability 10, distribution leverage 10, commercial potential 10.
- Signals preserve source URL/date and confidence as KNOWN, INFERRED, or ESTIMATED. No inferred relationship, client count, ACV, ARR, or deadline is represented as known fact.
- Award signals cluster by known funding program/funder; hiring signals by organization; community pain by theme; partner multipliers by partner.
- Distribution nodes and edges preserve public evidence. A partner with unknown client reach is explicitly scored below an evidenced multiplier; no client count is invented.
- The engine creates founder-approval-required playbooks only. It recommends Direct, Partner, and free-tool hooks where evidence fits; it never executes them.
- State is durable in the private gtm/opportunity-engine record and founder decisions survive subsequent reconciliation.
- Existing daily GTM and Partner reconciliation paths refresh the view after their normal work. The new view is isolated so an opportunity refresh failure cannot break Partner replenishment.
- Protected GET/PATCH APIs require GTM admin authentication. PATCH accepts only a validated cluster ID and REVIEW/APPROVED/SNOOZED/REJECTED status; it cannot perform campaign or send operations.
- The GTM Command Center now includes an Opportunity Radar / dedicated Opportunities queue with 5–10 decision cards, reason-now, evidence drawer, buyer-ready count, distribution routes, playbook, and Approve/Snooze/Reject decisions.
