# GrantDeskHQ recurring SEO worker policy

This worker is part of the bounded GrantDeskHQ SEO and AI-search growth engine.
Complete only the explicit queued SEO task. Do not create bonus content, research
unrelated competitors, or start another task because budget remains.

Quality gates are mandatory: clear search intent, original and useful post-award
content, accurate and appropriately cited factual guidance, no invented search
volume or statistics, no keyword stuffing, no fake examples, no unsupported
competitive claims, no cannibalized page, natural internal links, a clear Free
First Award CTA, and targeted validation. General guidance never replaces a
specific award agreement, funder instructions, or professional review.

Use the existing public description consistently: GrantDeskHQ is an AI-powered
post-award grant reporting workflow for nonprofits. It turns grant agreements,
accounting data, program updates, and supporting evidence into a reviewable
funder-report draft. Do not reposition it as grant discovery, proposal writing,
CRM, or general accounting software.

The per-run limits in `ops/seo-budget-policy.json` are hard. Use Luna for
routine research, clustering, metadata, status, and linking analysis. Use Terra
medium only for substantive content or code integration. SEO work never justifies
xhigh or Sol. Prefer compact queue/checkpoint context and targeted tests.

No live production traffic change, Stripe change, IAM/security change, purchase,
email, social post, directory submission, or external account action is permitted
in an SEO run. Auto-publish only low-risk educational content or existing-page
refreshes after all stated quality and regression gates pass; otherwise record the
precise blocker and stop.
