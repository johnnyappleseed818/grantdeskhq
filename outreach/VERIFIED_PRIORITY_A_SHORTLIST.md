## Outreach boundary

- Resend: explicitly opted-in contacts only.
- Public professional profiles: research and personalization only unless a separate outreach channel permits a compliant one-to-one commercial message.
- A one-time commercial message is still covered by CAN-SPAM. It must use accurate headers and subject lines, identify its commercial purpose, include a valid postal address and clear opt-out method, and honor opt-outs promptly.
- Obtain legal advice before launching cold outreach, particularly if recipients or organizations may be outside the United States.
